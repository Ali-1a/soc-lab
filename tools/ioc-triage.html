IOC Triage

Paste logs, a phishing email, or a threat report. The tool pulls out every indicator, defangs it, removes duplicates, and ranks it by the Pyramid of Pain — so you know what to block first and how much it hurts the attacker.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere, so it is safe for sensitive text. Input is automatically re-fanged (hxxp, [.]) before parsing.

No IOCs yet. Paste some text and press Extract IOCs, or load the example.

The Pyramid of Pain (David Bianco, 2013) ranks indicators by how hard they are for an adversary to change. Blocking a hash or IP is trivial for them to work around; detecting their tools and behaviour (TTPs) is what really hurts. This tool rates atomic indicators only — the top tiers (Tools, TTPs) come from analysis, not from parsing text.
